## Short description

Pollrr turns audience questions into trustworthy, reusable content. Creators publish a poll in seconds, distribute compact tracked links, and receive live human results, qualitative explanations, and a public verification record. Pollrr AI can improve question neutrality and summarize verified results, but AI-generated estimates are never mixed with human votes.

## Full description

Pollrr is public-opinion infrastructure for creators, publishers, and organizations that want more than a disposable social poll. Respondents answer before seeing the live split, reducing bandwagon effects in the experience. Every result identifies its human sample size, recruitment context, and methodology. Votes feed append-only evidence and signed aggregate snapshots, while optional explanations reveal why different groups answered as they did and where opposing groups share common ground.

Creating polls and collecting human responses is free. Pollrr’s paid layer is AI assistance for question design, follow-up ideas, verified result summaries, and distribution copy. AI interprets the record; it never invents respondents, changes totals, or silently blends modeled estimates into human results.

## Why creators care

- Audience participation becomes a repeatable content format.
- Results generate follow-up posts, videos, newsletters, and discussions.
- Compact links are easy to distribute and can carry channel attribution.
- Answer-before-results creates a stronger reveal than ordinary social polls.
- Transparent evidence gives serious creators something more credible to cite.
- The core workflow is free, with no response-limit paywall planned at launch.

## Three story angles

### The creator angle

Creators have rented audiences on social platforms. Pollrr helps them build first-party knowledge about what their communities think, while giving the audience a reason to return for the result.

### The trust angle

Most online polls publish a number without a receipt. Pollrr exposes human sample size, recruitment context, methodology, integrity handling, and signed aggregate evidence without releasing respondent identities or valuable row-level data.

### The AI angle

Pollrr draws a bright line: AI can help ask and explain; only humans can vote. Modeled estimates, if introduced later, will remain visibly separate from measured human responses.

## Claims discipline

Use “immutable” for recorded evidence and signed aggregate snapshots, not as a blanket promise that every database field can never change. Use “transparent” and “auditable” only when the linked public record demonstrates the claim. Describe open-link results as participating respondents or a convenience sample unless a documented sampling design supports a population estimate.
