# Pollrr — five creator pilot drafts

Date: August 9, 2026  
Status: approval-ready; no outreach sent.

These are invitations to critique a one-question pilot, not claims of adoption or representative polling.

## 1. Voices of Chandler

Your show is built around people shaping Chandler, which makes it a good place to test one transparent audience question. Pollrr lets people answer before seeing results and clearly labels whose audience participated. Would you be open to choosing one question from an upcoming episode and critiquing the result format with me? I would not present it as a citywide or representative poll.

Route: https://www.chandleraz.gov/government/departments/communications-and-public-affairs/voices-of-chandler

## 2. Mesa Chamber Podcast

I am building Pollrr as a human-first way for creators and community publishers to ask one audience question without turning the result into a claim about the general public. A Mesa business question—such as which administrative workflow consumes the most owner time—could be a practical pilot. Would the podcast team be willing to review a one-question format before anything is published?

Route: https://business.mesachamber.org/list/Find (Mesa Chamber podcast link)

## 3. East Valley Unite

Your local newsletter audience is a strong fit for a transparent reader pulse. Pollrr requires participants to answer before seeing results and can label the exact distribution source. I would like to propose one nonpartisan East Valley question and publish the limitations beside the result. Would you be open to reviewing the wording and disclosure first?

Route: https://www.eastvalleyunite.org/get-started

## 4. KRDP Community Radio

Community radio creates conversations that do not need to pretend to be scientific surveys. Pollrr could support one on-air or newsletter question, show the participating audience’s answers, and state plainly that the result is not representative of Arizona. Would KRDP be willing to critique a small pilot tied to one public-affairs segment?

Route: https://desertsoulmedia.org/krdp-community-radio/

## 5. State 48 News

I am testing a one-question tool for independent publishers: people answer before seeing results, only human votes count, and the methodology identifies the participating audience. I would like to offer State 48 News one local-business or quality-of-life question and a transparent results follow-up. Would you be open to reviewing the pilot before deciding whether to publish it?

Route: https://www.thestate48news.com/about
