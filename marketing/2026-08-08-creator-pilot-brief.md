# Pollrr creator pilot brief — August 8, 2026

Status: researched and approval-ready; no outreach sent.

## The one-question pilot

Invite a small Arizona creator or publisher to run one timely audience question. Participants answer before seeing results. Pollrr supplies a clearly labeled methodology, a source-specific distribution link, and a results view the creator can explain to the audience. AI may help ask or explain; only humans vote.

Pilot targets—not claims—are: one creator publishes one question, at least 25 completed responses are collected, the creator publishes a short results follow-up, and both sides decide whether a second question is warranted.

## Suggested live questions

- Which recurring city service creates the most friction for East Valley residents?
- What should local businesses disclose when an AI system participates in customer service?
- Which practical use of AI would most help a small local business: scheduling, customer follow-up, document work, or financial operations?

Every pitch should explicitly say the result reflects the participating audience, not the wider electorate or population.
