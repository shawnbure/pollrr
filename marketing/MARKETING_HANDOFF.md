# Pollrr marketing handoff

Updated August 5, 2026 (America/Phoenix)

## Product positioning

Pollrr is the trust layer for online opinion: a human-first polling and audience-intelligence platform where people answer before seeing results, human votes remain separate from AI analysis, and published results carry methodology and verification evidence. Poll creation and human-response collection are intended to be free; the initial paid opportunity is creator-facing AI assistance.

## Intended audience

- Independent political, public-policy, economics, technology, culture, and local-news creators
- Newsletter writers, podcasters, and publishers seeking recurring audience participation
- Civic organizations and small publishers that lack an internal research operation
- Later: researchers and organizations needing transparent longitudinal opinion data

## Brand voice

Clear, rigorous, human-first, skeptical of synthetic consensus, and confident without overstating scientific validity. Use US English. Explain limitations in plain language. Prefer “better receipts,” “human-only totals,” and “AI may help ask and explain, but only humans can vote.” Do not lead with “another polling app,” “AI polling,” or “the Palantir of polling.”

## Claims that are safe to make publicly

- Pollrr lets creators create and distribute polls.
- Respondents answer before seeing the live result.
- Pollrr distinguishes measured human responses from AI-generated analysis or modeled estimates.
- Pollrr publishes methodology and privacy information.
- Pollrr is designed for transparent, auditable aggregate results without publishing respondent identities or protected row-level data.
- AI can assist with question wording and result interpretation; it is not counted as a voter.
- Open-link results describe participating respondents or a convenience sample unless a documented sampling design supports a broader estimate.
- Creating polls and collecting human responses is currently free, if that remains true in the live product at the time of publication.

## Claims or facts that still need verification

- Do not call all results “immutable.” Confirm that the specific result has append-only evidence and a signed aggregate snapshot, then describe that evidence precisely.
- Do not claim Pollrr prevents bots, duplicate voting, manipulation, or coordinated campaigns. Describe integrity controls and flags without promising perfect prevention.
- Do not claim statistical representativeness, election accuracy, population inference, or causal findings without a documented sampling/experimental design.
- Do not claim creator adoption, engagement lifts, retention, revenue, testimonials, partnerships, press coverage, or first-party audience growth until measured and documented.
- Common-ground discovery, longitudinal opinion maps, AI summaries, source attribution, and public verification must be tested end to end before being described as generally available.
- “No response-limit paywall planned at launch” is a plan, not a permanent guarantee.
- Trademark availability and ownership of `POLLRR` are not settled. The July 28, 2026 preliminary search must be rerun before filing, and the application has not been filed.
- Verify dedicated social account ownership, handles, and account access before naming them as official Pollrr channels.
