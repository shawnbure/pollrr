# Pollrr publisher pilot scorecard — August 10, 2026

Status: internal pilot asset. No outreach was sent.

## Purpose

Use this scorecard to decide whether a creator or publisher is a strong first Pollrr pilot. The goal is not audience size. The goal is a credible question, an identifiable human audience, and a creator willing to show the limits of the result.

## Qualification score (0–2 each)

1. **Active audience:** recent original publication within 30 days.
2. **Question fit:** regularly asks readers for judgment, experience, or priorities.
3. **Trust fit:** willing to state that participation is self-selected and not representative polling.
4. **Conversation fit:** can publish the result and discuss what it does and does not mean.
5. **Pilot speed:** can run one question within 14 days without custom development.

Eight or more points: priority pilot. Five to seven: nurture. Below five: do not pursue now.

## Required pilot record

- Creator and public channel
- Exact question and response options
- Open and close timestamps
- Human-only voting rule explained
- Distribution route recorded
- Total verified participants reported without implying representativeness
- Creator follow-up: what surprised them, what they would change, and whether the result informed a real editorial or community decision
