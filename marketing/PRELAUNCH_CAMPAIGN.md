# Pollrr pre-launch campaign

## The position

**Pollrr is the trust layer for online opinion.** Creators and publishers can run free, answer-before-results polls while Pollrr preserves human vote totals, methodology, source provenance, explanations, and verifiable aggregate records. AI helps write neutral questions and interpret verified results; it never manufactures respondents or alters human totals.

Avoid leading with “another polling app,” “AI polling,” or comparisons to Palantir. Lead with the tension: online polls are useful content, but audiences cannot tell whether the question was fair, the respondents were real, or the result was manipulated.

## Who we want first

1. Independent political, local-news, economics, and public-policy creators.
2. Newsletter writers and podcasters who need recurring audience participation.
3. Local and nonprofit publishers that cannot afford a research operation.
4. Civic-tech practitioners interested in transparent methodology.
5. Product builders interested in verifiable, human-first AI.

## The creator promise

- Create and distribute a poll free.
- Make the audience participate before seeing the crowd.
- Turn one question into a post, a follow-up, a results story, and a discussion.
- Compare tracked distribution channels without blending them together.
- Publish a result with a visible human sample size and an auditable record.
- Use AI to improve wording and explain results—not to create fake votes.

## Thirty-day noisy-online sequence

### Week 1 — Establish the story

- Publish “The internet has enough opinions. It needs better receipts.” on Pollrr’s own site.
- Post the founder thread from `SOCIAL_COPY.md` on LinkedIn and X.
- Invite 10–20 small creators to run one real audience question at no cost.
- Submit Pollrr to the [Civic Tech Field Guide](https://civictech.guide/submit-listing/) as a tool/platform.
- Create founder profiles on Product Hunt and Indie Hackers, but do not schedule the major launch yet.

### Week 2 — Let creators make the proof

- Run five creator polls across different topics.
- Publish a compact weekly “What audiences actually said” report with sample sizes, recruitment labels, source splits, and links to verification records.
- Ask each pilot creator for one sentence about ease, engagement, or audience learning. Do not ask for generic praise.
- Post one transparent build note: what Pollrr flags, what it does not claim, and why AI estimates stay separate.

### Week 3 — Earn niche coverage

- Pitch creator-economy outlets with the audience-engagement angle.
- Pitch civic-tech and journalism outlets with the auditable-public-opinion angle.
- Offer a live demonstration built around a question chosen by the writer—not a canned demo.
- Publish the AI/human-separation article from `BLOG_POST_AI_HUMANS.md`.

### Week 4 — Coordinated preview

- Submit to BetaList once a public signup flow and several real creator polls are live. BetaList permits a pre-launch feature and a later launch feature, but requires something visitors can engage with.
- Post a candid Indie Hackers build story.
- Prepare Product Hunt assets, but launch only after the public product is stable. Product Hunt explicitly rejects vaporware and warns against paid hunting or manipulated traffic.
- Consider Show HN only when anyone can try the product immediately and the post can explain the technical trust model honestly.

## Outreach rule

Every pitch should contain one concrete observation, one Pollrr distinction, one link that works without login, and one small request. Never mass-send identical copy. Never claim representativeness for an open convenience sample. Never buy votes, upvotes, reviews, or “coverage.”

## What success looks like before launch

- 20 creator accounts that have published at least one poll.
- 10 polls with 100+ human responses each.
- 5 creator quotes tied to a specific outcome.
- 3 independent mentions, listings, interviews, or write-ups.
- 1 recurring public report that makes Pollrr’s transparency visible.
- A baseline of signup → publish, link → completed vote, and creator repeat-use rates.
