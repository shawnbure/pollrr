### One-sentence description

Pollrr is human-first public-opinion infrastructure that lets creators and publishers run free, answer-before-results polls with transparent methodology, human-only vote totals, qualitative explanations, and verifiable aggregate records.

### Full description

Pollrr helps creators, publishers, civic organizations, and researchers ask clear public questions and publish results with better evidence than a disposable social poll. Respondents answer before seeing the live split, reducing direct bandwagon influence in the voting experience. Pollrr preserves the human sample size, question version, recruitment source, methodology, integrity handling, and signed aggregate evidence so a published result can be checked later.

Respondents may optionally explain what mattered most. These qualitative explanations help show the reasoning on each side and identify areas of common ground without exposing respondent identities. Pollrr keeps measured human responses strictly separate from AI-generated analysis or modeled estimates: AI may improve question neutrality, organize themes, and summarize verified results, but it never creates votes or changes human totals.

Open-link polls are labeled as participating respondents or convenience samples unless a documented sampling design supports a population estimate. Public verification exposes enough information to understand how a result was produced without releasing identifying respondent information or protected row-level data.

### Message

Hi — I’m Shawn Bure, CEO of workrr.ai and founder of Pollrr.

Pollrr is a free polling and audience-intelligence platform built around one bright line: AI may help ask and explain, but only humans can vote. Respondents commit before seeing the live result; recruitment sources and sample limitations remain visible; optional explanations show the reasoning behind each side; and signed aggregate records make published totals checkable without exposing respondent identities.

The story is not another poll generator. It is whether creators and independent publishers can become useful first-party audience researchers without reproducing the black-box weaknesses of social polls—or replacing people with synthetic respondents.

I can offer a live demonstration using a question chosen by your publication and show exactly what Pollrr does and does not claim.
